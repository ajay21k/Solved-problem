Problem A — Sensitive data exposure

Customer records may be exposed through compromised systems, misconfigured storage, unauthorised access or third-party systems.

Problem B — Delayed breach detection

Traditional monitoring may fail to recognise suspicious access patterns or the gradual extraction of sensitive records.

Problem C — Data correlation and re-identification

Individual fields that appear harmless may become sensitive when combined across multiple datasets.

Problem D — Containment after suspicious access

A security system may detect an anomaly but fail to determine which records are at risk and what proportionate protective action to take.


# Privacy-Aware Adaptive Breach Containment System

AI-assisted cybersecurity · Data leak prevention · Adaptive access control

A system that detects suspicious access to sensitive customer records, estimates which data is at risk, identifies relationships between exposed fields, and dynamically applies the least disruptive protective action.

The research question

Can a system combine access-behaviour analysis, sensitive-data relationship mapping and risk-based containment to reduce data exposure while producing fewer false alarms than conventional detection methods?
