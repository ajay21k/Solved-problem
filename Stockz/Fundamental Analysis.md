1) EPS - Earning per share

For example company A and company B
 A makes  50 Rs for 1 share
 B makes 75 Rs for 1 share

Now obviously B is better

2)  P/E ratio - Price to Earnings

if company A need 100 Rs to make 10 Rs  and B needs 200 Rs to make 10 Rs

Now A is better than B.

3) Dept to Equity ratio 
if company A relies on 50% on dept and B relies on 10% 

No B is better

4) ROE - return on equity 
Simple return on invest if A makes 10% profit and B makes 8% then 

Now A is better

5) Market Cap 
If Market Cap is high then possibility of loss is high

6) Book Value 
Book value if share price is 50Rs .But the company has dept to pay and salary to pay. The book value should be nearest to share price.

7) PB - Price to book
Is similar to ex-show room price and on-road price. The price to book should be less.

Candles 

Bullish - Up trend 

 Bearish - Down trend

1) Maruboze (Green)- If open is low and close is hight (Then it is considered as good)
2) Pin Bar(Green) - It shows the possible of  uptrend after a long downtrend
3) Hanging man (red)- If red hammer in top , possibility of down trend
4) Inverted hammer(Green) - Possibility for uptrend but not strong as Pin bar 
5) Shooting start Inverted hammer hanging man(red) - Higher probability for down trend
6) Spinning top (red or green) - Equal space in top and bottom and small
7) Doji - No body = open == close
			1) Open == low == close == high
			2) Open == low == close (Cementry doji)
			3) open == high == close(T doji or dragon fly)

Multiple candle stick

1) Engalphing - If a candle can swallow previous candle .It can occur for (red and blue) then trend reversal posible
2) Harami - if large comes previous to small then trend reversal posiblie . but less posibility than engalphing
3) Piercing - if a candle starts at the half of the previous candle then trend reversal possible
